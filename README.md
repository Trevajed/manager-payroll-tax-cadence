# Manager Payroll Tax Cadence Extension

Read-only Manager.io extension showing payroll taxes accrued, paid and owed by month, quarter and year, with on-screen Form 941, Form 940 and Oregon Form OQ/132 worksheets built from your ledger.

Extensions for [Manager.io](https://www.manager.io), published by Two Sisters Nightmare, LLC. Licensed under MIT (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).

| Extension | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Payroll Tax Cadence](payroll-tax-cadence/) | Read-only view of accrued, paid and owed payroll taxes by month, quarter and year, with on-screen Form 941, Form 940 and Oregon Form OQ / 132 worksheets. |
| [payroll_account_balances_extension.html](payroll_account_balances_extension.html) | Earlier extension: balances of payroll liability accounts. |

Each extension is a single HTML file you paste into Manager (Settings → Custom Buttons).

Not tax or legal advice. Worksheets are for transcribing figures, not filing.
